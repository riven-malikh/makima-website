Makima - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

1. Overview

Makima is a Discord security and moderation bot. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when Makima is used in a Discord server.

2. Information Makima May Process

Depending on the features enabled by a server, Makima may process Discord information necessary to provide security and moderation features. This may include Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, message information, and moderation-related events.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide security and moderation features, operate the bot, maintain functionality, and respond to moderation events.

4. Data Storage

Data storage depends on the features enabled and how Makima is configured. Makima does not intentionally store information that is unnecessary for its functionality.

5. Data Sharing

Makima does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by services necessary to operate the bot, where applicable.

6. Data Removal

Server administrators may contact the Makima development team to request deletion of applicable data associated with their server.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by Makima. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Makima's features or data practices change.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Makima development team through the official support server or contact method provided by the development team.
